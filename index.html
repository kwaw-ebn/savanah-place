1. Executive Summary

Overall Assessment

Following a comprehensive audit of Savannah Place Assisted Living Facility’s digital presence across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and website channels, it is evident that the organization has invested significant effort into content publishing and brand consistency. However, despite consistent posting activity, the current social media ecosystem is underperforming severely in terms of audience growth, engagement, visibility, community trust-building, and lead conversion.

The organization currently demonstrates a “content publishing” strategy rather than a true “audience growth and engagement” strategy.

While the visual branding is moderately consistent and the messaging demonstrates empathy and care-oriented positioning, the content lacks the emotional authenticity, platform optimization, community interaction, and conversion-focused storytelling required to perform effectively within the assisted living and senior care industry.

Strategic Insight:
The current strategy is producing minimal business impact and weak digital authority.

5. Key Problems Identified

1. Posting Without Audience Strategy

The organization is posting consistently but not building audience ecosystems. Posting frequency alone does not create growth. Growth requires engagement loops, audience targeting, platform optimization, and strategic distribution.

2. Lack of Human-Centered Storytelling

Families choose assisted living facilities based on emotional trust signals. Current content lacks warmth, authenticity, emotional transformation stories, and real human connection.

3. Overuse of Static Graphics

Most posts resemble promotional flyers rather than social-native content optimized for engagement.

4. Weak Local Community Integration

No evidence of strong partnerships, community networking, or local engagement initiatives.

5. Weak Conversion Funnel

There is no structured journey guiding users from awareness to inquiry and ultimately to facility tours and conversion.

6. Strategic Recommendations

Content Strategy Recommendations

  • Shift toward human-centered storytelling.
  • Introduce resident lifestyle content.
  • Implement weekly video and reels strategy.
  • Use authentic photography over flyer-style graphics.
  • Create family testimonial campaigns.
  • Increase caregiver spotlight content.

Paid Advertising Strategy

  • Launch Facebook lead generation campaigns.
  • Implement local awareness campaigns.
  • Retarget website visitors with conversion ads.
  • Target adults aged 40–65+ in Holly Hill and surrounding areas.

Google Business Profile Optimization

  • Weekly photo uploads.
  • Regular video uploads.
  • Review generation strategy.
  • Local keyword optimization.
  • Improved FAQ structure.

9. Final Strategic Conclusion

Savannah Place Assisted Living Facility possesses strong foundational brand potential but currently lacks a modern audience-growth and engagement strategy.

The organization’s current digital presence is operational but not strategically optimized for trust-building, emotional conversion, audience acquisition, and local authority positioning.

Final Recommendation:

Savannah Place should prioritize becoming a trusted caregiving community rather than simply a facility posting graphics online. Long-term success will depend on:
  • Authentic storytelling
  • Video-first marketing
  • Community engagement
  • Trust-centered branding
  • Conversion-focused campaigns
