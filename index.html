Digital Marketing & Social Media Audit Report

Savannah Place Assisted Living Facility
Executive-Level Brand Growth & Social Media Evaluation

Executive Summary

Following a comprehensive audit of Savannah Place Assisted Living Facility’s digital presence, it was identified that while the organization has maintained consistent posting activity across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, the brand currently experiences extremely low engagement, weak discoverability, minimal audience growth, and poor community interaction.

The current digital strategy reflects a “content publishing approach” rather than a true audience growth and trust-building ecosystem.

Social Media Audit

Facebook Analysis

  • Consistent posting frequency but extremely low engagement.
  • Heavy use of text-heavy graphics affecting reach.
  • Lack of community interaction and emotional storytelling.
  • No evidence of paid amplification campaigns.

Instagram Analysis

  • Minimal reels and short-form video strategy.
  • Low discoverability and weak platform adaptation.
  • Overly corporate visuals reduce relatability.

LinkedIn Analysis

  • Minimal professional authority positioning.
  • No healthcare leadership storytelling.
  • Weak employer branding strategy.

Strategic Recommendations

Recommended Focus Areas

  • Transition to human-centered storytelling.
  • Implement short-form video strategy.
  • Launch local Facebook advertising campaigns.
  • Increase caregiver and resident-focused content.
  • Improve Google Business Profile optimization.
  • Build community partnerships and engagement.
  • Create stronger conversion-focused CTAs.
